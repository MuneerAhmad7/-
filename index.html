3-Time Free Nutritious Meals

Hygenic, well-balanced fresh meals cooked daily in our modern institutional kitchen for all full-time boarders.

Free Hostel Lodging (*Qiyam*)

Clean, ventilated dormitory halls equipped with comfortable beds, laundry facilities, and medical support.

Strict Discipline & Mentorship

اخلاقی تربیت و سرپرستی

24/7 dedicated residential wardens ensuring character building, punctuality in prayers, and moral discipline.

اساتذہ کی چوبیس گھنٹے نگرنی میں طلباء کی دینی و اخلاقی تربیت اور نمازوں کی پابندی۔

24/7 CCTV Camera Monitoring System

24 گھنٹے سی سی ٹی وی کیمروں کی نگرانی

To ensure maximum safety, campus grounds, dormitories, computer labs, and entry gates are actively monitored around the clock by high-definition security cameras.
